## Background

For a few months now, I have been struggling to find motivation to write. Primarily because, with the rise of social media, the addition of image and short video formats, and the rise of podcasts and long-form audio, the world has become a lot more fast-paced and driven by content consumption rather than thinking, reflecting and creating content. I feel the value of content has decreased as the number of people creating content has increased dramatically. However, this should not become a continuing blocker for me, so I need to write down and document the demotivating factors and the potential motivating factors to overcome the roadblocks to continue writing.

## Problems

### Social media

I think this has become the biggest demotivator. With the advent of social media, the amount of content generated has been so large that I myself consume a large amount of posts, articles, podcasts, videos, and audiobooks every day. While I have experimented with deleting quite a few social media apps to cut down on scrolling and reading comments, I still end up spending about 3-4 hours per day consuming content. This is unsustainable, unproductive, and very shallow, short-term consumption of content.

Furthermore, the endless comments and opinions dilute the value of my opinion in my own eyes. I see so many smart people creating original audio-visual content or aptly identifying problems, sharing their opinions and solutions in a precise few words, and I question what I would achieve by writing something about it and just adding to the noise.

### Anger, frustration and helplessness

Content consumption causes anger because of various factors: whether someone did something wrong or unjust, I had expectations from someone (say government, other organizations or individuals) and their actions didn't meet my expectations, etc. This further causes frustration and helplessness when I don't see any quick solutions to any of the problems. This again demotivates me from expressing my opinions via writing as I don't see any way out of systemic issues.

### Long-term viability concerns

I also always think about the long-term viability of my writing and whether it will be beneficial for others or even myself in the future. Oftentimes, while writing mid-way I feel that something may not be relevant a few months or years down the line and then give up.

### Quest for perfection and fear of criticism
Another problem is that I constantly worry about how others will perceive my writing. I fear criticism and negative feedback, which makes me hesitant to share my thoughts. The more invisible I am, the more immune I will be from any criticism and negative feedback.

## Motivation

This is the section that I will come back to in the future to remind myself why I started writing in the first place and what my goals are. I will keep updating this section as I go through my life journey and hopefully it will keep motivating me to continue writing.

* I write to document my thoughts and ideas primarily for my future self. I hope like-minded people will find this blog helpful as they navigate through their own life journeys. However, that would be a bonus as there is no expectation of that from my side.
* My writings might become totally irrelevant or obsolete in the future, but that's okay. The act of writing itself is valuable and helps me process and record my thoughts and ideas. I want to keep a record of my journey and growth throughout my lifetime because life is nothing without memories.
* Writing gives me the perfect platform to keep learning and trying out new things in life so that I can write about it and keep myself motivated about life as well.
* I want to expand this blog to include different types of content with the use of technology. It will also be a record of the evolution of technology and means of communication, content creation, knowledge sharing, and consumption.
* If I ever get some financial benefits from this blog, that would be a nice bonus, as I have always thought that if I were not in the technology world, I would have been in finance.
* Finally, this is a reminder for me to keep reducing social media and short-term consumption of content and focus on long-term self-development and progress.
